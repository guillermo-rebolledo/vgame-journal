# shelve.games

A personal record of games played, games being played, and games to play later.

## Language

**Journal**:
Your saved game record, including each game's Status, optional Completion tag, and Playthroughs.
_Avoid_: Diary, inventory, Library when referring to the whole record.

**Library**:
The view of all games saved in your Journal, including Wishlist games.
_Avoid_: Owned games, collection when naming this destination.

**Status**:
A game's current place in your Journal: Played, Playing, Backlog, or Wishlist. Each saved game has exactly one Status, chosen by you; release dates do not change it automatically.
_Avoid_: Progress, tag, shelf when referring to Status.

**Played**:
The Status for a game you have played and are not currently playing. It does not necessarily mean you finished it.
_Avoid_: Finished, Completed as synonyms for Played.

**Playing**:
The Status for a game you are currently playing.
_Avoid_: In progress, active when naming this Status.

**Backlog**:
The Status for a game queued to play later.
_Avoid_: Queue, to-do, owned as substitute Status labels.

**Wishlist**:
The Status for a game you want to play, whether released or upcoming; also the view grouping those games by release. Wishlist games are part of your Library.
_Avoid_: Unreleased games, shopping list, favorites.

**Completion tag**:
An optional description of how you left a Played game: Completed, Retired, Shelved, or Abandoned. It supplements Played rather than replacing the Status.
_Avoid_: Status, achievement, custom tag.

**Playthrough**:
A dated record of playing a game on a platform, with a start date and optional finish date, hours, and private notes. A game may have several Playthroughs; a finish date alone does not change its Status or Completion tag.
_Avoid_: Session, review, Journal entry when naming a Playthrough.

**Rating**:
Your half-star score, from 0.5 to 5, for a game in your Journal, given while its Status is Played or Playing. One per game; it stays through later Status changes. It is separate from a Completion tag and a Playthrough, and rating never changes either or your Status.
_Avoid_: Score, review, User score when naming a Rating. Reserve "IGDB user score" for the external Catalog value.

**Community rating**:
The average of all shelve.games players' Ratings for a game, shown once it reaches the Community rating threshold.
_Avoid_: User score, IGDB user score, critic score.

**Countdown**:
The time remaining until a Wishlisted game's known release, following your selected Release region. An unknown release date is shown as unannounced rather than as a numeric countdown.
_Avoid_: Timer, launch alarm.

**Release region**:
Your preference for which regional release dates Countdowns follow, with Earliest worldwide as the default.
_Avoid_: Location, timezone when naming this preference.

**Discovery**:
The selection of Catalog games offered to help you find something to play. Appearing here does not add a game to your Journal.
_Avoid_: Personal recommendations when no personalization is provided.

**Catalog**:
The games and game information available to search and browse, including games outside your Journal.
_Avoid_: Library when referring to all searchable games.
