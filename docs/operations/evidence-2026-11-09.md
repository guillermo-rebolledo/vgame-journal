### What these measurements support

- **Journal size is not what moves the save.** Warm save p95 is 1055, 1062 and 1062ms at 0, 100 and 1000 games with 5 users — flat, and inside the provisional 2-second budget, as are the cached page reads at ≤532ms. The first pass appeared to show a 1000-game save regression at p95 2888ms; that number came from one sample spanning both the save and a whole-Journal export read. Measured apart, the export read is 62/135ms (p50/p95) at 1000 games and the save is unchanged. Concurrency, not dataset, dominates.
- **No lost write was observed anywhere in the matrix.** Pairing each `journal-save` with the `journal-export-verify` of the same user, round and mode: across all six cells there is **no** case of a save that acknowledged and then failed its read-back. The 100/20 cell recorded 11 saves that never acknowledged inside the deadline; 6 of those read back as not written, which is the control correctly refusing to confirm, and **5 read back as written without a confirmed acknowledgement**. That last group is the interesting one and belongs to [#140](https://github.com/guillermo-rebolledo/vgame-journal/issues/140): a player retrying such a save must not create a duplicate. It is not evidence of data loss.
- **The 20-user ceiling observed here is the harness environment's, not production's.** All 159 failures across the three 20-user cells are on the `slow-network` pass; the warm pass recorded none. 137 are `shell` or `marker`, meaning the landmark or its content never rendered inside the deadline. The remaining 22 are not navigation: 11 `status-control` saves that never acknowledged, 6 `export-correctness` read-backs and 5 `interactive` readiness budgets exhausted, all in the 100/20 cell. Saving and reading back gave way under this load too, not only rendering. One local Node process serving 20 concurrent throttled clients runs out of render throughput; `slow-network` p95 reaches 22708ms for Home and 23175ms for Library in the 100/20 cell. Do not read a dataset trend across the 20-user cells: 100/20 failed 100 samples while 1000/20 failed 34, on a shared development machine. 20 users explores a ceiling, it does not establish a supported limit.
- **Readiness is a real player-facing characteristic.** At 5 users on 150ms/1.6Mbps the Status control needs p50 ~2.42s and p95 ~2.7s before it answers, and it took exactly 3 taps in every successful slow-network sample of all three 5-user cells — the first two were discarded — against p50 ~0.32s warm. A player tapping Status before that has their tap discarded with no feedback. Recorded for the application maintainer; no UI change was made under this issue.
- **The HTTP error counter is explained.** All 1849–2997 errors per cell are 404, and are the two scripts the platform serves only on a Vercel deployment, `/_vercel/insights/script.js` and `/_vercel/speed-insights/script.js` — two per page load, identified 2026-11-09 by request inspection. No application 4xx or 5xx was recorded. Earlier passes reported this counter without saying what it contained.
